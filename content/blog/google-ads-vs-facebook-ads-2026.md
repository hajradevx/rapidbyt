"Should I run Google Ads or Facebook Ads?" is one of the most common questions from business owners trying paid advertising for the first time.

The wrong answer costs you thousands of rupees. The right answer depends entirely on your business type, your target customer's behavior, and your marketing stage. Most generic advice gets this wrong because it treats the platforms as interchangeable.

Here's the honest breakdown — including Pakistan-specific context most guides ignore entirely.

---

## The Fundamental Difference (This Changes Everything)

Before comparing costs or features, understand what each platform is actually doing:

|                  | Google Ads                          | Facebook Ads                           |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **User intent**  | Active searching                    | Passive browsing                       |
| **User mindset** | "I need this right now"             | "Oh, that's interesting"               |
| **Demand type**  | Captures existing demand            | Creates new demand                     |
| **Best for**     | People who know they want something | People who don't know they want it yet |

**Google Ads** — someone types "AC repair Karachi" → your ad appears → they click because they need AC repair right now. The intent is already there. You're just capturing it.

**Facebook/Instagram Ads** — someone scrolling their feed → your ad appears → they might click if it's visually compelling. No prior intent. You're creating the desire.

Neither is better. They serve fundamentally different purposes. Choosing wrong means spending money at the wrong stage of your customer's journey.

---

## How Businesses Use Each Platform

**Google Ads is best when:**

- Your product/service is something people search for when they need it
- Customers have clear intent when they're ready to buy
- You can capture people at the bottom of the buying funnel

**Facebook/Instagram Ads is best when:**

- People don't know your product exists yet
- Your product is visually compelling and benefits from images/video
- You want to reach a specific demographic or interest group
- You're building brand awareness before people start searching

**The most powerful approach:** Use both together. Facebook creates awareness. Google captures the people that awareness generates. Many Pakistani businesses treating these as an either/or choice are missing a significant multiplier effect.

## Cost Comparison in Pakistan (2026 Actual Data)

Cost data for Pakistani ad accounts varies significantly by niche and time of year. These are realistic ranges based on campaigns we manage and monitor.

### Google Ads Costs in Pakistan

| Metric                                 | Range             |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Average CPC (low competition)          | PKR 50–200        |
| Average CPC (medium competition)       | PKR 200–600       |
| Average CPC (high competition)         | PKR 600–2,500     |
| Minimum daily budget to see data       | PKR 500–1,000     |

**High-cost niches in Pakistan (CPC):**

- Insurance, loans, banking: PKR 1,000–2,500/click
- Real estate: PKR 500–1,800/click
- Legal services: PKR 600–2,000/click
- Medical/dental: PKR 400–1,200/click

**Lower-cost niches in Pakistan (CPC):**

- Home services (plumber, electrician): PKR 40–150/click
- Education/tutoring: PKR 60–250/click
- Food and restaurants: PKR 30–100/click
- Clothing and fashion: PKR 60–200/click

### Facebook/Instagram Ads Costs in Pakistan

| Metric                                 | Range                              |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Average CPM (per 1,000 impressions)    | PKR 150–700                        |
| Average CPC                            | PKR 15–120                         |
| Average CPL (cost per lead form)       | PKR 300–1,500                      |
| Minimum daily budget                   | PKR 200 (but PKR 800+ recommended) |
| Monthly minimum for meaningful testing | PKR 8,000–15,000                   |

**Why Facebook is cheaper per click in Pakistan:**
Facebook's ad inventory in Pakistan is large relative to advertiser demand. Many Pakistani advertisers don't run Facebook Ads or run them poorly — meaning less competition for ad space. This creates an opportunity: quality Facebook ads can reach large Pakistani audiences at relatively low costs.

**The key caveat:** Cheaper clicks from Facebook are lower-intent than Google clicks. A PKR 80 Facebook click that converts at 1% is equivalent to a PKR 400 Google click that converts at 5%. Calculate **cost per acquisition** (CPA), not cost per click.

## When Google Ads Wins: Specific Use Cases

### High-Intent Local Services

When people have a problem and search for a solution:

- "emergency plumber Lahore" → call immediately
- "dentist near me Karachi" → book appointment today
- "laptop repair Islamabad" → bring it in this week

These searches have immediate, high-intent buyers. The conversion window is short and the searcher is already ready. Google Ads puts you in front of them at exactly the right moment.

### Established Product Categories

When people already know they want the product category:

- "buy iPhone 15 Pakistan" — they've decided to buy, just choosing where
- "office chairs price Lahore" — comparing prices to purchase
- "Urdu online course" — researching specific known products

### B2B Services

Business owners searching for specific business solutions:

- "payroll software Pakistan"
- "web development company Karachi"
- "accounting firm SME Lahore"

B2B buyers research extensively before purchasing. Google Ads captures them at the research and decision stages.

### Remarketing After Someone Visits Your Site

People who visited your website but didn't convert are warm leads. Google Display Network remarketing shows your ads to them across websites they browse. This is often the highest-ROI use of Google Ads budget for service businesses.

## When Facebook/Instagram Ads Win: Specific Use Cases

### New Products Nobody Is Searching For

If no one searches for your product, Google Ads shows to nobody. You need to create awareness first. Fashion brands launching new styles, food businesses launching new products, software tools that solve problems people don't know they have — these need Facebook's proactive reach.

### Visually-Driven Products

Fashion, food, home decor, beauty, lifestyle products — these convert dramatically better with video and image ads than with text-based search ads. Instagram Stories and Reels can showcase a product in ways that search ads can't.

### Pakistani Market Reach

Facebook and Instagram have massive penetration in Pakistan's urban and semi-urban markets. The ability to target by:

- City (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, Faisalabad)
- Age, gender, income indicators
- Interests (specific to Pakistani user behavior)
- Job titles and professional status (LinkedIn-like targeting on Facebook)

This geographic and demographic precision is Facebook's unique strength for the Pakistani market.

### Building Brand Awareness Before Ads Get Expensive

If you're entering a niche where Google Ads CPC is very high (insurance, finance, real estate), build brand awareness with cheaper Facebook ads first. When people later search on Google, they recognize your brand — improving your click-through rate and potentially your Quality Score (which reduces CPC).

### Retargeting: The Highest ROI Facebook Use Case

Facebook pixel on your website + Facebook retargeting = showing ads only to people who've already shown interest. Cost per conversion on retargeted audiences is typically 3–7× lower than cold traffic.

Install the Facebook pixel on your site if you haven't. Every visitor gets cookied. Run a retargeting campaign to this warm audience. This is often the first Facebook campaign that clearly pays for itself.

## The Pakistani Market Reality: What's Different Here

**Facebook still dominates in Pakistan** — Facebook and Instagram combined have significantly higher usage among Pakistani consumers than in most Western markets. WhatsApp Business integration with Facebook Ads also creates unique remarketing opportunities.

**Google search volume is lower** than Western markets for many niches — meaning less competition and sometimes lower CPCs, but also a smaller potential search audience for certain keywords.

**Payment method for ads:** Both Google and Facebook accept international Visa/Mastercard. If you don't have one, you'll need to work with a digital marketing agency that runs ads on your behalf. Some local payment methods work via Google Ads prepaid billing — check the current options in your Google Ads billing settings.

**Mobile-first audience:** Pakistan's internet users are overwhelmingly mobile-first. Both platforms optimize for mobile, but ensure your landing pages are also mobile-optimized — a slow or broken mobile page wastes your entire ad budget. Run our [free diagnosis tool](/diagnose) to verify your mobile performance before spending on ads.

**Trust dynamics:** Pakistani consumers often research on Google but are influenced by social proof on Facebook (group recommendations, page reviews, shared content). A combined strategy — Google for intent capture, Facebook for social proof and awareness — leverages both trust mechanisms.

## How to Allocate Budget

### Starting Out (PKR 15,000–25,000/month)

Don't split your budget between both platforms at this stage. Neither platform will have enough data to optimize properly with a split budget.

**Choose one based on your business type:**

- Service business or B2B → Start with Google Ads
- Product business or consumer brand → Start with Facebook/Instagram

Spend the full budget on one platform for 30–60 days. Measure cost per lead or cost per acquisition. Only add the second platform once you understand your conversion economics on the first.

### Growing Budget (PKR 50,000–100,000/month)

Now you can test both:

- 60% of budget on the primary platform
- 30% on the secondary platform
- 10% on retargeting campaigns

Review monthly. Shift budget toward whichever platform delivers the lower cost per acquisition for your goals.

### Scale (PKR 100,000+/month)

Run both platforms simultaneously with platform-specific creative:

- Google: Text ads focused on intent keywords, Shopping ads for e-commerce
- Facebook/Instagram: Video and carousel creative for awareness, static image for retargeting
- Separate remarketing campaigns on both platforms for website visitors

## The Biggest Mistakes on Each Platform

### Google Ads Mistakes

**Broad match keywords without negatives:**
Bidding on "website" with broad match shows your ad to everyone searching anything with the word "website" — including "website templates free download" and "how to make a website yourself." Add extensive negative keyword lists before spending.

**Homepage as the landing page:**
Sending all ad traffic to your homepage kills conversions. Every ad group needs a dedicated landing page matching the exact search intent. For the full landing page optimization guide, see the [landing page conversion fix guide](/blog/traffic-not-converting-landing-page-fixes).

**Stopping campaigns too early:**
Google Ads uses machine learning to optimize. It needs 2–4 weeks and at least 50 conversions to exit the "learning phase." Pausing campaigns during this window resets the algorithm. Commit to at least 30 days of consistent spend before evaluating.

**Ignoring Quality Score:**
Quality Score (1–10) affects your ad rank and CPC. A higher QS means better placement for less spend. Improve QS by: matching ad copy closely to the keyword, improving landing page relevance, and improving expected CTR through compelling ad copy.

### Facebook Ads Mistakes

**Audience too narrow:**
Under 100,000 people in your target audience limits delivery and increases CPM. Broaden your targeting or use Advantage+ Audience (Facebook's AI targeting) for small markets like individual Pakistani cities.

**Changing ads too frequently:**
Facebook's algorithm needs 5–7 days to exit the learning phase per ad set. Changing targeting, budget, or creative resets the learning phase. Run ads for at least 7 days before making changes.

**No retargeting:**
Your hottest audience — people who visited your website — is wasting if you have no retargeting campaign. Install the Facebook pixel, create a website custom audience, and run a separate retargeting campaign. This is typically 3–5× more cost-efficient than cold traffic.

**Ignoring ad fatigue:**
The same audience sees your creative repeatedly. After 2–3 weeks, frequency (average number of times each person has seen your ad) rises and performance drops. Refresh creative every 2–3 weeks or rotate multiple variations.

## Measuring Success: What to Track

**Don't optimize for clicks — optimize for outcomes:**

| Metric                         | What It Tells You                                    |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Click-Through Rate (CTR)       | Ad relevance — is your creative/copy compelling?     |
| Cost Per Click (CPC)           | Competitiveness of your targeting and quality        |
| **Conversion Rate**            | Landing page effectiveness                           |
| **Cost Per Lead (CPL)**        | How efficiently you're generating inquiries          |
| **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** | The number that actually matters — cost per customer |
| Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)      | Revenue generated per rupee spent — for e-commerce   |

If your clicks are cheap but conversions are zero, the problem is your landing page — not your ads. If conversions are high but your CPA is too high for profitability, increase conversion rate or reduce CPC.

## Our Recommendation for Pakistani Businesses

**For most Pakistani local service businesses (doctor, plumber, repair, tuition):**

> Start with **Google Ads**. High-intent local search captures ready buyers. Budget PKR 20,000–30,000/month to test properly.

**For Pakistani product businesses (clothing, food, consumer goods):**

> Start with **Facebook/Instagram Ads**. Cheap CPM, massive Pakistani audience, visual format matches these products.

**For any business with existing website traffic but no ad campaigns:**

> Start with **Facebook retargeting** — target your website visitors. This is the cheapest possible first paid campaign because the audience is already warm. Takes 30 minutes to set up.

**If budget is very limited:**

> There are effective ways to [get organic traffic without paid ads](/blog/get-first-1000-visitors-without-paid-ads) first — build that foundation while saving for a proper ad budget.

## One Truth Both Platforms Share

Regardless of which platform you choose: if your website is slow and your landing page doesn't convert, ad spend is wasted.

A visitor clicking your ad and landing on a page that takes 6 seconds to load will leave before seeing your offer. Google's own data shows a 1-second delay in mobile load time reduces conversions by up to 20%. On a PKR 30,000/month ad budget, that's PKR 6,000 evaporating into slow load times.

Before running ads, verify:

- Mobile PageSpeed score above 70 (test at pagespeed.web.dev)
- Clear call-to-action visible above the fold
- No layout shifts that confuse users on mobile
