"Should I run Google Ads or Facebook Ads?" is one of the most common questions from business owners trying paid advertising for the first time.

The wrong answer costs you thousands of rupees. The right answer depends entirely on your business — and most generic advice gets this wrong.

Here's the honest breakdown.

---

## The Fundamental Difference

This is the most important thing to understand:

|                 | Google Ads        | Facebook Ads                 |
| --------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **User intent** | Active searching  | Passive browsing             |
| **Mindset**     | "I want this now" | "Oh, that looks interesting" |
| **Best for**    | Capturing demand  | Creating demand              |

**Google Ads** = someone types "AC repair Karachi" → your ad appears → they call you. High intent, ready to buy.

**Facebook Ads** = someone scrolling their feed → your ad appears → they might click. Low intent, needs convincing.

Neither is better — they serve different purposes.

## Cost Comparison (Pakistan Market 2026)

### Google Ads Costs

| Metric                           | Value                 |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Average CPC (Pakistan)           | PKR 50 – 500          |
| High-competition niches          | PKR 500 – 2,000/click |

**Expensive niches in Pakistan:**

- Insurance, loans, banking: PKR 1,000-2,000/click
- Real estate: PKR 500-1,500/click

**Affordable niches:**

- Local services (plumber, electrician): PKR 50-200/click
- E-commerce products: PKR 100-400/click
- Education: PKR 80-300/click

### Facebook Ads Costs

| Metric                           | Value                                |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Average CPM (Pakistan)           | PKR 200 – 800 per 1,000 impressions  |
| Average CPC                      | PKR 20 – 150                         |
| Minimum daily budget             | PKR 200 (but PKR 1,000+ recommended) |
| Monthly minimum to test properly | PKR 10,000 – 20,000                  |

Facebook is generally cheaper per click in Pakistan — but the clicks are lower intent.

## When Google Ads Wins

### 1. High-Intent Services

People search when they have an immediate problem:

- "emergency plumber Lahore"
- "dentist near me Karachi"
- "laptop repair Islamabad"

If someone is searching for your service, Google Ads puts you in front of them at exactly the right moment.

### 2. Established Product Categories

When people already know they want the product:

- "buy iPhone 15 Pakistan"
- "office chair price Karachi"
- "Urdu course online"

### 3. B2B Services

Business owners searching for solutions:

- "accounting software Pakistan"
- "web development company"
- "digital marketing agency Karachi"

### 4. High Lifetime Value Customers

When one customer is worth PKR 50,000+, paying PKR 2,000 per click is justified if conversion rate is reasonable.

---

## When Facebook Ads Wins

### 1. New/Unknown Products

If no one is searching for your product yet, Google Ads won't work — there's no search volume. Facebook lets you show the product to targeted people who don't know they want it yet.

### 2. Impulse Purchases

- Fashion, clothing, accessories
- Food and restaurants
- Entertainment and events
- Beauty products

### 3. Building Brand Awareness

For new businesses that need to get their name known before people will search for them.

### 4. Remarketing

People who visited your website but didn't buy — Facebook retargeting is highly effective and cheap.

### 5. Detailed Audience Targeting

Facebook knows demographics, interests, behaviours. You can target:

- "Women aged 25-35 in Karachi interested in skincare"
- "Business owners in Lahore who use QuickBooks"

Google targets keywords — Facebook targets people.

## The Pakistani Market Reality

A few things work differently in Pakistan:

**Facebook still dominates social media** in Pakistan — much higher penetration than in Western markets. Facebook and Instagram ads reach a massive Pakistani audience cheaply.

**Google search volume is lower** than Western markets for many niches — meaning less competition and sometimes lower CPCs, but also a smaller potential audience.

**Payment issues:** Both platforms accept international cards. If you don't have a Visa/Mastercard, running paid ads is difficult. Some agencies offer to run ads on your behalf using their accounts.

**Trust factor:** Pakistani consumers often research on Google but buy based on social proof (Facebook/Instagram reviews, WhatsApp recommendations). A combined approach works best.

## How to Split Your Budget

**If you have PKR 20,000/month:**

Starting out — pick one platform first. Don't split PKR 20,000 into two PKR 10,000 budgets — neither will be enough to gather meaningful data.

**Choose based on your business:**

- Service business, B2B → Start with Google Ads
- Product business, consumer → Start with Facebook Ads

**If you have PKR 50,000+/month:**

Now you can test both:

- 60% on your primary platform
- 40% on the secondary
- Review after 30 days, shift budget to what's working

## The Biggest Mistakes People Make

### With Google Ads:

1. **Broad match keywords** — "shoes" instead of "buy running shoes Karachi" — you'll burn through budget on irrelevant clicks
2. **No negative keywords** — add words that should NOT trigger your ad
3. **Sending traffic to homepage** — always send to a specific landing page
4. **Stopping too early** — Google Ads needs 2-4 weeks to optimise

### With Facebook Ads:

1. **Too narrow audience** — under 100,000 people limits delivery
2. **Changing ads too often** — let them run for at least 7 days before judging
3. **No retargeting** — the warmest audience (website visitors) is the most valuable
4. **Ignoring ad fatigue** — refresh creatives every 2-3 weeks

---

## Our Recommendation

**For most Pakistani businesses starting with paid ads:**

> Start with **Facebook Ads** — lower minimum budget, easier to learn, Pakistani audience is large and engaged. Once you understand conversion tracking and landing pages, add Google Ads for high-intent traffic.

**Exception:** If you're a local service business (doctor, plumber, lawyer, repair shop), start with **Google Ads** — the intent is immediately there and conversions happen faster.

## One Thing Both Platforms Agree On

Regardless of which platform you choose — if your website is slow, your ad spend is wasted.

A visitor clicking your ad and landing on a page that takes 5 seconds to load will bounce before they convert. Google's own data shows that a 1-second delay in mobile load time reduces conversions by up to 20%.

Before spending on ads, make sure your landing page:

- Loads in under 2 seconds on mobile
- Has a clear call-to-action above the fold
- Doesn't have layout shifts that confuse users
